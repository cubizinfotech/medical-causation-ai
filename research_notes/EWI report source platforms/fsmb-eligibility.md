# FSMB PDC eligibility, lawful substitutes, and Gemini 3 vs 2.5 grounding (accessed 27 October 2026)

Access date for all page reads unless a source is dated otherwise: **27 October 2026**. Currency: **USD**. Unpublished list prices are marked **quote-only**. This note resolves a purchasing conflict for a **US personal-injury law firm** (not a hospital): do not budget or recommend FSMB Physician Data Center (PDC) Standard ($9) or Premium ($12) as a buyable SKU. Earlier internal notes that treated PDC Premium as Package B’s license/discipline line should be overridden on eligibility, not on the published $9/$12 list price.

---

## Who is eligible for FSMB PDC according to FSMB official pages and terms (hospitals, credentialing orgs, law firms, plaintiff vs defense)?

### Takeaway
FSMB sells PDC Query Services to **hospitals, credentialers, and other health-care organizations**, not to law firms. Official pages never list attorneys, plaintiff firms, or defense firms as eligible customers. Plaintiff vs defense is irrelevant: neither side is a named PDC buyer class.

### Cited Findings
- The live PDC account-request form opens with: “If you’re a **hospital or health care organization** and would like to request a PDC account, fill out the form below….” Physicians are sent to Practitioner Direct; patients are sent to free DocInfo. Law firms are not mentioned. — [FSMB PDC Query Form](https://inte.fsmb.org/PDC/pdc-query/pdc-query-form/)
- The PDC home page describes PDC as FSMB’s data-sharing tool that **state medical boards** use to share licensure/discipline, and that “**credentialers, hospitals, and other organizations**” use to access verified data on more than one million licensed physicians and PAs. Query Services: “Search for verified information on an individual physician or PA….” Data Files: bulk licensure/disciplinary data “complete with key identifiers like NPI.” — [FSMB Physician Data Center](https://www.fsmb.org/pdc/)
- PDC Query marketing copy: “The platform is built to support the **day-to-day work of credentialing teams** and the state medical boards that contribute the underlying data.” Audience language is credentialing, not litigation. — [FSMB PDC Query](https://www.fsmb.org/PDC/pdc-query)
- During the expired March 2020 COVID no-cost window, FSMB’s own statement (republished by ACGME) listed who “must be” a qualified PDC user: **Hospital; Government Agency; Credentials Verification Service; Managed Care Facility; Placement Service; Medical Group or Clinical Practice.** Attorneys and law firms are not on that list. — [ACGME / FSMB statement, March 2020](https://www.acgme.org/newsroom/2020/3/acgme-shares-federation-of-state-medical-boards-fsmb-information-on-access-to-physician-data-center/)
- Published per-query prices (still on the live FAQ on 27 Oct 2026): **Standard Profile $9.00/physician** including 12 months of monitoring; **Premium Profile $12.00/physician** = Standard plus ABMS board specialty. Charged when a query is run; monthly invoice on the first of the month; pay by card or check within 30 days; one org account, unlimited users. Queryable professions: MD, DO, PA only. Profiles **do not contain malpractice information**. — [FSMB PDC FAQ](https://www.fsmb.org/PDC/pdc-faq/)
- Standard vs Premium field table: both include provider name, medical school, graduation year, degree, state-board sanctions, federal sanctions, licensure verification, NPI; **only Premium** includes specialty board certifications. FSMB calls PDC a designated equivalent source by ABMS. — [FSMB PDC Query](https://www.fsmb.org/PDC/pdc-query)
- PDC FAQ: meets Joint Commission and URAC primary-source-equivalent requirements for state-board and federal sanctions; NCQA-approved source for state-board and federal sanctions history. All state medical boards contribute; cadence daily/weekly/monthly/quarterly. Disciplinary alerts received daily. Account setup: “Click here to set up and register for a new account” (the old `/PDC/request-a-pdc-account/` URL returned **HTTP 404** on 27 Oct 2026). Contact: `pdc@fsmb.org`, phone (817) 868-4010. — [FSMB PDC FAQ](https://www.fsmb.org/PDC/pdc-faq/)
- The site Terms of Use Agreement (last revised **15 January 2014**) is a generic FSMB website contract. It requires an account to purchase Products, monthly invoicing, 30-day payment, and Texas law/arbitration. It **does not list eligible entity types**. It does prohibit robots, spiders, “data mine,” and reproducing the Service. — [FSMB Terms of Use](https://www.fsmb.org/pdc/pdc-tos)
- A separate “PDC Data Files” intake exists for bulk/file options “to augment your physician data collection or to streamline your credentialing process.” No USD price. — [FSMB PDC Data Files form](https://production.fsmb.org/PDC/pdc-data-files/pdc-data-form/)

### Inferences
- On every current FSMB marketing and intake page, the buyer is a **credentialing organization**. A US PI law firm investigating an opposing expert is not that buyer, whether plaintiff or defense.
- The $9/$12 figures are real public list prices **for eligible customers**. They are not a catalog item a law firm can add to a cart. Treating them as EWI Package B cost is a purchasing error.
- The 2014 TOS does not create a secret attorney SKU. It also forbids scraping DocInfo/PDC as a workaround.
- A Credentials Verification Service (CVO) **is** on the 2020 eligible-party list. Whether a CVO hired by a law firm (rather than by a hospital) would be approved is unpublished; do not assume yes.

### Gaps
- FSMB has not published a yes/no for: PI law firm, expert-research vendor, software vendor powering law-firm reports, or a CVO whose only client is a law firm. The only honest next step is a written answer from `pdc@fsmb.org`, not an order.
- Data-licensing / bulk-file USD prices are unpublished (**quote-only**).
- Whether FSMB would reject an application that truthfully lists “law firm” vs accept one that misstates the org as a “health care organization” is a compliance question; the public copy does not authorize the second path.

## Is there any FSMB product sold to attorneys?

### Takeaway
**No current FSMB product is sold to attorneys for investigating another physician.** DocInfo is the free public/consumer lookup. Practitioner Direct and FCVS are physician-paid (or physician-initiated) credentialing tools. PDC and data licensing are institutional credentialing products. There is no plaintiff vs defense FSMB litigation SKU.

### Cited Findings
- DocInfo (`www.docinfo.org`) is FSMB’s **free** consumer tool drawing on the Physician Data Center. The 19 August 2015 relaunch said consumers enter name and state and receive: whether the physician has been disciplined, states of active licensure, medical school, city/state, and specialty certification from ABMS and AOA, with a link to the state board for sanction details; it also covers physician assistants. — [FSMB news release, 19 Aug 2015](https://www.fsmb.org/siteassets/advocacy/news-releases/2015/news-release-docinfo-relaunch.pdf)
- The PDC query form still points patients to “our free online resource, DocInfo.” — [FSMB PDC Query Form](https://inte.fsmb.org/PDC/pdc-query/pdc-query-form/)
- DocInfo how-to (updated 16 July 2024) is a name/state search with no fee step; support is `docinfo@fsmb.org`. — [DocInfo Zendesk: How do I search for a doctor?](https://docinfo.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/28080178033428-How-do-I-search-for-a-doctor)
- **Practitioner Direct** lets a **physician** send their own PDC Profile (and, for participating boards, an NPDB report) electronically to a state medical board. “There is no fee to submit either report through Practitioner Direct.” A physician may also request a self-query of their own PDC Profile. This is not a third-party attorney query. — [FSMB Practitioner Direct](https://www.fsmb.org/PDC/practitioner-direct/)
- **Federation Credentials Verification Service (FCVS)** is a lifetime repository of verified core credentials that **physicians and PAs** establish for state licensure, hospital privileges, employment, and memberships. Physician **initial application $395** (portfolio + one profile to one recipient); subsequent profiles **$99** each, or **$65** each when requested with an Initial or Subsequent application. PA initial **$170**; subsequent **$65**. Extra fees: ECFMG education verification **$75** (IMG, one-time); ECFMG Certification Status Report **$70** per state-board designation; NPDB-via-FSMB **$3.50** for designated boards. Payment is by the applicant in the FCVS application (Visa/MC/Amex, US billing address). FCVS does not advertise attorney purchase of another person’s profile. — [FSMB FCVS](https://www.fsmb.org/fcvs/); [FSMB FCVS Cost and Fees](https://www.fsmb.org/fcvs/cost-and-fees)
- FSMB site TOS forbids robots/spiders/data-mining of the Service. — [FSMB Terms of Use](https://www.fsmb.org/pdc/pdc-tos)
- A third-party “DocInfo (FSMB) Physician Lookup API” exists as an unofficial wrapper over docinfo.org; the vendor itself says FSMB does not publish a public DocInfo developer API. That is not an FSMB product. — [Parse marketplace listing](https://parse.bot/marketplace/f0b243ce-2fbd-42f1-a7ca-7d8f98a62d80/docinfo-org-api)
- Official PDC **API** exists for PDC customers (OAuth2, production `https://services-pdc.fsmb.org`) after a signed agreement and an existing PDC account. That API is not a public attorney API. — [github.com/fsmb/pdc-api](https://github.com/fsmb/pdc-api) (referenced from prior catalog work; not re-fetched as an eligibility source)

### Inferences
- The lawful FSMB path for a PI firm is **manual DocInfo use by a human**, then confirmation at the issuing state board. It is not an API, not a citable PSV artifact of the same class as PDC, and not something to scrape.
- FCVS $395/$99 is the wrong SKU: the **physician** pays to send *their own* verified credentials to a board or hospital. An opposing expert will not sell you that file, and FSMB does not sell it to counsel.
- Practitioner Direct $0 is likewise the physician’s self-service channel, not a litigation query.
- Do not buy unofficial DocInfo scrapers. They collide with FSMB’s robot prohibition and are not primary source.

### Gaps
- No FSMB “for attorneys” product page was found on 27 Oct 2026.
- Historical consumer DocInfo fees (a 2001 press account of ~$9.95) are obsolete after the 2015 free relaunch; they are not a current attorney SKU.
- Whether a physician’s FCVS Self-Designation PDF, if the expert voluntarily produced it in discovery, could be *used* by counsel is a document-in-hand question, not a product FSMB sells to firms.

## ABMS CertiFACTS: are attorneys an intended customer? Any public USD price or only quotes?

### Takeaway
**Yes — attorneys are an intended CertiFACTS customer class.** ABMS Solutions expressly names “hospitals and health systems as well as **attorneys, insurance groups, and other professionals**.” **No public USD price** exists as of 27 Oct 2026; it is **quote-only** (Limited plan minimum 5 queries, or Unlimited 1- or 2-year). Certification Matters is free for patients and **not authorized** for professional/EWI use.

### Cited Findings
- ABMS Solutions FAQ, “Verifying a physician’s certification (for professional use)”: products “allow hospitals and health systems as well as **attorneys, insurance groups, and other professionals** to check physicians’ records quickly, conveniently, and securely.” Offerings: annual subscriptions, flexible query packages, and linkages into credentialing platforms. Data can include certification effective/expiration dates, historical certifications, and continuing certification (formerly MOC). — [ABMS Solutions FAQs](https://www.abmssolutions.com/faqs/)
- CertiFACTS Online plans: **Limited** (any number of searches, **minimum 5 queries**); **Unlimited** one-year and two-year subscriptions. Unlimited plans get “My Favorite Physicians” lists and alerts. Daily updates from 24 ABMS Member Boards; >1 million physicians. Profiles meet TJC/NCQA/URAC PSV. “Certifacts does not provide any third-party webcrawl access or software integration.” No USD prices on the product page. Sales: (800) 733-2267, `sales@ABMSSolutions.com`. — [CertiFACTS Online](https://www.abmssolutions.com/product-comparison/certifacts-online/)
- ABMS Direct Connect Select is API access **only through named credentialing platforms** (Axuall, HealthStream, MD Staff, symplr, Verifiable on current product pages; an older FAQ still names Cactus/MDStaff/CredentialSmart/Vistar). Not a law-firm self-serve API. No USD prices. — [ABMS Solutions FAQs](https://www.abmssolutions.com/faqs/); [ABMS Direct Connect Select](https://www.abmssolutions.com/product-comparison/abms-direct-connect-select/)
- “Permitted use of certification data”: “Please refer to your signed legal agreement… or contact ABMS Solutions at (800) 733-2267.” Sharing printed ABMS data is contract-governed, not a public license. — [ABMS Solutions FAQs](https://www.abmssolutions.com/faqs/)
- **Certification Matters** (`certificationmatters.org`) is for patients/families. “If you need to verify the credentials of a doctor for any professional purposes… information obtained through the ABMS Certification Matters website is intended for patient use only and is **not valid or authorized for use by professional organizations**.” Professional users are told to contact ABMS Solutions. — [Certification Matters patient vs professional FAQ](https://www.certificationmatters.org/faq/what-is-the-difference-between-a-patient-and-a-professional-user/)
- A New York Workers’ Compensation Board bid notice for “WCB Renewal of CertiFACTS Online” (bid date 17 Oct 2025) described “CertiFACTS subscription renewal 1 year License plus 3 additional users, Coverage Term: 11/27/25–11/26/2026” but **did not publish the dollar amount**. That is evidence of a 1-year seat product, not a list price. — [NYSCR / newyorkbids.net WCB notice](https://www.newyorkbids.net/government-agencies/schenectady/workers-compensation-board-807625/14448518-wcb-renewal-of-certifacts-online.html)
- Generic 2026 credentialing-cost blogs that say “ABMS member boards typically charge $25 to $75 for verification letters” are **not** CertiFACTS list prices and should not be used as a substitute quote. — [PayerReady 2026 credentialing-cost post](https://payerready.com/blog/how-much-does-credentialing-cost-per-provider-2026-breakdown) (aggregator; not ABMS)

### Inferences
- For an MD expert, **CertiFACTS Limited (min 5 queries)** is the professional product a PI firm can actually be sold, unlike PDC. Until sales quotes a number, budget it as **quote-only**, not as $12 PDC Premium.
- Do not use Certification Matters (or Google snippets of it) in an EWI credential section. ABMS forbids professional use.
- Direct Connect Select is useless unless the product is rebuilt on a named credentialing platform. CertiFACTS’ “no third-party webcrawl or software integration” note is a warning against caching CertiFACTS HTML into this app.

### Gaps
- CertiFACTS Limited per-query USD price and Unlimited annual USD price are unpublished as of 27 Oct 2026 (**quote-only** from 800-733-2267 / sales@ABMSSolutions.com).
- Whether CertiFACTS will license a software vendor that stores results for many law-firm reports (vs a law firm using the UI) is unpublished; the no-integration note cuts against an adapter.
- The WCB 2025–26 renewal dollar amount was not in the public bid snippet.

## AOA Official Osteopathic Physician Profile as a DO substitute (eligibility + 2026 USD)

### Takeaway
AOA Profiles has a **published $25 per profile** price for nonmember physicians and for listed healthcare organizations, but **attorneys/law firms are not on the healthcare-organization type list**. Do not treat $25 as a confirmed law-firm SKU. It is a lawful DO-certification source **if** AOIA will sell to the firm or a qualifying CVO; otherwise it is the physician’s own $25 self-order, not something counsel can buy about an opposing expert.

### Cited Findings
- Pricing FAQ: AOA members send or order Profiles at **no cost**. All other orders: **Physician, Nonmember of AOA: $25 per Profile**; **Healthcare Organization: $25 per Profile** ($25 also for mail or phone orders). Charges are not refundable. Cards: Amex/Discover/MC/Visa. — [AOA Profiles FAQ](https://aoaprofiles.org/faq); [AOIA AOA Profiles](https://www.aoiassn.org/aoa-profiles)
- Healthcare organization types (click-through list): Physician Group Practice; Group or Ambulatory Surgical Center; HMO; Hospital; Hospital Network; IPA; Physician Sponsored Network; MCO; Physician Recruiting Firm (that performs credentials verification for its “own” physicians or those applying for privileges/membership); Practice Management Firm; PPO. **Law firm / attorney is not listed.** — [AOA Profiles healthcare organization types](https://www.aoaprofiles.org/healthcare-organization-types); [AOA Profiles register](https://www.aoaprofiles.org/register)
- Registration split: Healthcare Entity vs Credentials Verification Organization vs College of Osteopathic Medicine. A CVO is defined as an organization that “conducts credentials verification, reports the credentialing information to clients and has systems in place to protect the confidentiality and integrity of the information.” — [AOA Profiles register](https://www.aoaprofiles.org/register)
- AOIA describes the service as primary source for Healthcare Organizations, CVOs, Colleges of Osteopathic Medicine, and State Boards. — [AOIA AOA Profiles](https://www.aoiassn.org/aoa-profiles)

### Inferences
- $25 is a real public price, unlike CertiFACTS, but **eligibility is the constraint**, as with PDC. A PI firm is not a listed HCO type.
- A CVO registration might be a path if the EWI vendor actually performs credentials verification for client firms and can meet AOIA’s confidentiality language. That is unpublished until AOIA says yes.
- A DO expert can order/send their own nonmember profile at $25. That is not an investigation purchase.

### Gaps
- Whether AOIA will sell to a law firm or to a software/CVO serving law firms is unpublished. Email `credentials@AOAprofiles.org` or phone (312) 202-8000 rather than assuming the $25 HCO rate applies.

## Official state-board bulk files for FL / CA / TX / NY / NJ as a substitute cost?

### Takeaway
For license + discipline, the lawful substitute for PDC is **issuing-state official lookup and/or official bulk/API**, not a national FSMB login. **Florida, California, Texas, and New York’s OPMC discipline file are $0 public data** (account or request form in some cases). **New Jersey** publishes a free bulk roster UI with **no fee printed on the page**. None of these is ABMS certification. None requires hospital credentials.

### Cited Findings

#### Florida
- MQA Search Services (HTML) is the public license/discipline search; the **Data Download Portal** is for large public datasets “best suited for business or research purposes,” updated **daily**, pipe-delimited ASCII, license status / mailing address / email; All Professions exceeds 1 million records. Sign-on is required so DOH can contact users; the User Guide does **not** list a download fee. Portal is described as Chapter 119 public data. — [MQA Search Services and Data Download User Guide, rev. 1.27.2025](https://mqa-internet.doh.state.fl.us/MQASearchServices/Content/HelpFile/MQA%20SearchServices%20and%20DataDownload%20UserGuide.pdf); [FL HealthSource Data Portal](https://flhealthsource.gov/data-portal/)
- Separate public-records copies of disciplinary/licensure **documents**: 50 pages or less **$0**; 51+ pages **$0.15/page** for the total; microfiche $0.62/page if over 15 pages; certification **$25** each; research time billed if over 30 minutes. That is document copies, not the bulk license file. — [Florida DOH licensure/disciplinary document request](https://www.floridahealth.gov/about-us/boards-councils-and-committees/professional-boards/request-for-disciplinary-and-licensure-documents/)

#### California
- Medical Board of California: **free** Microsoft Access 2007+ physician/surgeon public database, refreshed every **Tuesday morning**; outpatient surgery settings monthly. Delivered via **BOX** after a request form (name, email, **Business/Organization**, dataset). Same information as DCA Search. No NPI; email/phone confidential. API: DCA iServices, real-time, best with a known license-number list; one-hour demo required after DCA creates the account; **no published API fee**. — [MBC Public Information](https://mbc.ca.gov/Resources/Statistics/Public-Information.aspx)
- Paper copies of public records: **10 cents/page** plus staff time under BPC §161; lists of licensees in other formats may have cost-recovery charges (phone 916-574-8004). The Access bulk DB itself is described as free. — [MBC PRA Guidelines](https://www.mbc.ca.gov/Download/Documents/public-records-act.pdf)
- A separate MBC Box login page states the system is “restricted to authorized users for legitimate **law enforcement and regulatory purposes**.” That banner is on `mbc.ca.gov/box/`, not on the Public Information bulk-request form, which is open to “interested parties.” Treat the Box warning as possibly a different/internal login, not as a published bar on attorney receipt of the public Access file. — [MBC Box login](https://www.mbc.ca.gov/box/); contrasted with [MBC Public Information](https://mbc.ca.gov/Resources/Statistics/Public-Information.aspx)

#### Texas
- Official bulk + API: Texas Open Data Portal **DataSet-01-All Licenses** (`tm3v-pfq9`), ~**514K** rows, 23 columns, updated **daily**; last updated **26 October 2026**. Fields include license type, name, year of birth, license number, issue/expiration, registration status, **disciplinary_status**, disciplinary_status_date, license_status. OData v2 advertised. **No fee** on the open-data page. Live lookup is also public (data last updated 26 Oct 2026 on TMB’s site in prior catalog work). — [data.texas.gov DataSet-01-All Licenses](https://data.texas.gov/dataset/DataSet-01-All-Licenses/tm3v-pfq9)
- Paid monthly Excel/text reports also exist via TMB Open Records Self-Service Portal (ORSSP). Price is shown only after login/checkout (**quote/cart-only**). Not required if the open-data file is used. — [TMB ORSSP](https://orssp.tmb.state.tx.us/) (not re-priced on this pass)

#### New York
- **Discipline (physicians/PAs/etc.):** Health Data NY dataset “Professional Medical Conduct Board Actions: Beginning 1990,” identifier `ebmi-8ctw`, **free** Socrata/OData. Last updated **2 October 2026**. Monthly posting of public OPMC actions since 1990. Fields: name, address, license number, license type, year of birth, effective date, action, misconduct description, date updated. Individual search remains on the DOH OPMC site. — [Health Data NY OPMC dataset](https://health.data.ny.gov/Health/Professional-Medical-Conduct-Board-Actions-Beginni/ebmi-8ctw)
- **License verification:** NYSED Office of the Professions public HTML search is free (name, profession, license number, location, original-license date, registration status). NYSED is “currently working on an API for bulk verification searches”; organizations may request an API key via support ticket; **no fee is stated** on the ticket form; access is not automatic. — [NYSED Bulk Verification Request](https://op-support.nysed.gov/hc/en-us/requests/new?ticket_form_id=7599671137683); [NYSED online verification help](https://www.op.nysed.gov/services/verifications/online-verification-searches)
- NYSED **physician licensure application fees** ($735 licensure, etc.) are what *doctors pay to become licensed*, not what a firm pays to verify. Do not budget them as investigation cost. — [NYSED physician license requirements](https://www.op.nysed.gov/professions/physicians/license-requirements)

#### New Jersey
- Official bulk roster UI: `https://newjersey.mylicense.com/Verification_Bulk/Search.aspx` — select profession, license type, status; “the download may take a few minutes.” The 27 Oct 2026 fetch showed **no USD price**. Live verification at MyLicense is “intended to be used for VERIFICATION ONLY,” with bulk users redirected to the roster download. — [NJ Verification_Bulk](https://newjersey.mylicense.com/Verification_Bulk/Search.aspx)

### Inferences
- A PI firm **can** lawfully acquire FL/CA/TX/NY-OPMC official files without being a hospital. That is the correct PDC substitute for multi-state license/discipline **coverage of those states**, plus HTML/API lookup for every other state on the expert’s license list.
- Dollar cost of the substitute stack for FL+CA+TX+NY discipline+NJ roster is **$0 list**, plus staff time and (only if certified paper copies are needed) FL $0.15/page or CA $0.10/page.
- This stack does **not** replace CertiFACTS for MD board-certification dates. PDC Premium’s ABMS specialty field is also not available if PDC cannot be purchased.
- NPI is absent from CA MBC bulk files; identity join still needs NPPES.

### Gaps
- Exact Florida download-file field list for a discipline **flag** vs status-only still needs a logged-in sample (User Guide lists status, mailing address, email; metadata file not re-fetched here).
- Whether NYSED’s bulk-verification API is actually issuing keys in 2026, and at what cost: form still says “working on.”
- NJ roster: no official fee on the page; a third-party 2026 map previously reported a **$0.00** confirmation screen. Treat NJ bulk as **no published fee**, almost certainly $0, but not as a screenshot of a 27 Oct 2026 checkout total.
- CA iServices: whether an attorney/research vendor will be approved is unpublished (form asks Business/Organization; no published fee).
- TMB ORSSP cart prices remain unpublished until checkout; not needed if open data is used.

## Gemini API grounding: current free quota and overage for the model this app uses (`gemini-3.5-flash-lite` / `gemini-embedding-2`), vs Gemini 2.5 Flash — official 2026 pages

### Takeaway
This app’s LLM (`gemini-3.5-flash-lite`) is a **Gemini 3** model. On a **paid** Gemini Developer API project it uses the **Gemini 3+ search meter: 5,000 free search requests per month** shared across all Gemini 3 and newer models, then **$14 per 1,000 search requests**. That is **not** the Gemini 2.5 Flash meter (**1,500 grounded prompts/day** shared with 2.5 Flash-Lite, then **$35 per 1,000 grounded prompts**). One prompt can bill **multiple** search queries. Embeddings (`gemini-embedding-2`) are **$0.20 per 1M text tokens** on paid Standard (free of charge on the free tier). Official pages last updated **27 October 2026 UTC** (Developer API pricing) and **13 October 2026 UTC** (grounding how-to).

### Cited Findings

#### What the code actually calls
- Repo defaults: `GEMINI_LLM_MODEL=gemini-3.5-flash-lite` and `AI_EMBEDDING_MODEL=gemini-embedding-2` in `.env.example`, `.env`, and `apps/api/src/ai/constants/ai-providers.constant.ts`. Grounding docs list **Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite** as a supported Google Search model. — local env/constants; [Grounding with Google Search (Interactions API)](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/google-search) (last updated **2026-10-13 UTC**)

#### Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite (this app) — Developer API
- Model card `gemini-3.5-flash-lite`, paid **Standard**: input **$0.30 / 1M** (text/image/video/audio); output **$2.50 / 1M** including thinking tokens. Free-tier tokens: “Free of charge.” Grounding with Google Search: free tier **“Not available”** (footnote: can be tested in Google AI Studio); paid **5,000 free search requests per month (shared across all Gemini 3 and newer models), then $14 per 1,000 requests.** Same $14 meter on Batch/Flex/Priority. Footnote: “A customer-submitted request to Gemini may result in one or more queries to Google Search. You will be charged for each individual search query performed.” — [Gemini Developer API pricing](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing) (**Last updated 2026-10-27 UTC**)

#### Gemini 3 vs 2.5 meters (same official pricing page + grounding how-to)
- Tools table, paid Google Search: **Gemini 2.5 models:** 1,500 RPD free (limit shared for Flash and Flash-Lite), then **$35 / 1,000 grounded prompts**. **Gemini 3 and newer models:** 5,000 free search requests per month (shared across all Gemini 3 and newer models), then **$14 per 1,000 requests**. Free-tier Google Search tool: **500 RPD** free (shared Flash and Flash-Lite); not available for Pro. — [Gemini Developer API pricing, Pricing for tools](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing)
- Gemini **2.5 Flash** (`gemini-2.5-flash`) paid Standard: input **$0.30 / 1M** (text/image/video), output **$2.50 / 1M**; grounding **1,500 RPD free** (shared with 2.5 Flash-Lite), then **$35 / 1,000 grounded prompts**. Free tier grounding up to **500 RPD** shared with Flash-Lite. — [same pricing page, Gemini 2.5 Flash](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing)
- How-to (13 Oct 2026): Gemini 3 and newer are billed **per search query the model executes**, with 5,000 free/month then $14/1,000. Example: two queries inside one prompt count as **two** billable uses; empty queries are ignored. **Gemini 2.5 or older is billed per prompt.** — [Grounding with Google Search](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/google-search)

#### Embeddings (this app)
- `gemini-embedding-2` paid Standard: **text input $0.20 / 1M tokens**; image $0.45 / 1M; audio $6.50 / 1M; video $12.00 / 1M. Free-tier text/image/audio/video input: Free of charge.
